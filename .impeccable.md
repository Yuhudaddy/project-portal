## Design Context

### Users
Field construction staff and general-contractor reviewers use the tool on a phone at the worksite. Their job is to enter one continuous-wall unit, verify key construction values, and immediately export a PDF that can be sent through LINE or saved on a computer.

### Brand Personality
Professional, calm, and dependable. The interface should feel like a practical construction record rather than a decorative consumer app.

### Aesthetic Direction
Mobile-first, restrained grayscale, strong hierarchy, high legibility in bright outdoor conditions, and compact data-entry sections. Avoid excessive color, black-filled panels, gradients, and ornamental cards.

### Design Principles
・ One construction unit, one in-memory record, one PDF; there is no save or sync function.
・ Use five compact tabs in two rows so the field workflow is visible without a long landing page.
・ Put record creation and PDF export within easy thumb reach.
・ Keep labels and metadata compact while preserving 16px form controls and 44px touch targets.
・ Never hide uncertainty; show missing measurements and negative differences explicitly.
