## Design Context

### Users
Field construction staff and general-contractor reviewers use the tool on a phone at the worksite. Their job is to record one continuous-wall unit, verify key construction values, and export a PDF that can be sent through LINE or saved on a computer.

### Brand Personality
Professional, calm, and dependable. The interface should feel like a practical construction record rather than a decorative consumer app.

### Aesthetic Direction
Mobile-first, restrained grayscale, strong hierarchy, high legibility in bright outdoor conditions, and compact data-entry sections. Avoid excessive color, black-filled panels, gradients, and ornamental cards.

### Design Principles
・ One construction unit, one record, one PDF.
・ Put the next field action and export action within easy thumb reach.
・ Keep the field workflow simple while preserving the detailed audit trail.
・ Use explicit states for draft, saved, measured, pending, and needs confirmation.
・ Never hide uncertainty; mark unreadable or unverified values instead of guessing.
